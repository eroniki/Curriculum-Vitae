\section{Research Profile}
%------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Educational psychologist and mixed-methods researcher studying motivation, engagement, and success of students in STEM higher education and graduate training. Combines advanced quantitative methods (structural equation modeling, multilevel modeling, longitudinal analysis, meta-analysis) with qualitative methods to identify the course- and institution-level conditions that help STEM students persist and succeed. Currently leads the quantitative research of a National Science Foundation (NSF) study of how graduate funding structures shape Ph.D.\ student success.
